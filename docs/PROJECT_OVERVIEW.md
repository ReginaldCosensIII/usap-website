## Fixed-scope summary

Within the 48 hours:

- Up to six approved product-family pages (exact count subject to client approval).
- An organized technical-resource library/static shell with approved documents, categories, and organization.
- Contact Us and Request a Quote forms with antiforgery, server-side validation, and SMTP delivery.
- Basic SEO metadata, GA4 integration, and a sitemap at the level documented in the proposal.

## Scope exclusions

Outside the 48-hour scope unless a written authorized addition is approved:

- More than six product-family pages.
- Advanced Technical Resources search or filtering (separately authorized — see above).
- Broad public-site-only content extraction when editable source is unavailable.
- Substantial copywriting or independent engineering-claim verification.
- Broad imagery sourcing, licensing coordination, a product-image library, or custom supporting graphics.
- Deep technical-document auditing, deduplication, redesign, or validity determinations.
- Expanded analytics, event/download tracking, Search Console work, or structured data beyond the baseline.
- Legal text drafting. Privacy Policy and Terms of Use routes may be created once approved legal text is supplied; the routes themselves are not categorically excluded.
- A database, CMS, SPA framework, frontend build pipeline, or microservices layer.

Any work outside fixed scope requires written approval and a tracker update before implementation begins.
